John 3

1
There was a Pharisee named Nicodemus, a respected leader on the Jewish ruling council.
2
He came to Jesus at night and acknowledged Him as a teacher sent from God because of the miraculous signs He performed.
3
Jesus told him that no one can see the kingdom of God without being born anew from above.
4
Nicodemus asked how a grown person could possibly be born a second time or re-enter their mother’s womb.
5
Jesus replied that entering the kingdom of God requires being born of water and the Spirit.
6
Physical birth produces physical life, whereas the Spirit gives birth to spiritual life.
7
So do not be surprised when I tell you that you must be born from above.
8
The wind blows wherever it wills; you hear its sound yet cannot trace where it begins or goes, and so it is with everyone born of the Spirit.
9
Nicodemus asked in astonishment how such things could happen.
10
Jesus answered, asking how a leading teacher of Israel could fail to understand these truths.
11
He testified that they spoke from firsthand knowledge, yet people still refused to receive their witness.
12
If you do not believe when I speak of earthly realities, how will you believe when I speak of heavenly things?
13
No one has gone up into heaven except the One who came down from heaven—the Son of Man.
14
Just as Moses lifted up the bronze serpent in the wilderness, so the Son of Man must be lifted up,
15
so that everyone who places their trust in Him may receive eternal life.
16
For God loved the world so deeply that He gave His one and only Son, so that whoever trusts in Him will not perish but have eternal life.
17
God did not send His Son into the world to condemn it, but so that the world might be rescued through Him.
18
Whoever trusts in Him is not condemned, while anyone who refuses to believe stands condemned already for rejecting God’s only Son.
19
This is the verdict: light has entered the world, yet people loved darkness rather than light because their deeds were evil.
20
Everyone who practices evil avoids the light for fear that their actions will be exposed.
21
Yet whoever lives by the truth steps gladly into the light, showing clearly that what they have done has been accomplished in God.
